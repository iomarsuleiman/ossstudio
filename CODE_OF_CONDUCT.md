# Code of conduct

OSS Studio welcomes contributors and visitors of every background and experience level. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, publishing personal information without consent, and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and museum public chat.

Report concerns privately through a maintainer's contact listed on their GitHub profile. If no private contact is available, ask for a reporting channel without posting sensitive details. Maintainers will review reports discreetly, hear relevant context and choose a proportionate response. They may remove content, warn participants, restrict access or ban repeated or severe misconduct.

Maintainers follow the same expectations. Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not lead to retaliation.
